description: Draft a follow-up email from a meeting's action items
---

Draft a follow-up email for the meeting: $ARGUMENTS

Read the meeting's `notes.md` (write it first via the `meeting-notes` skill if
it does not exist yet).

Draft an email that:

- Thanks them briefly. One line, not a paragraph.
- States what was agreed, so the record is shared rather than private.
- Lists their action items and the user's separately, with dates.
- Names the specific next step and who moves first.

Match the register of how the participants actually spoke in the transcript.
A call full of jokes should not produce a stiff corporate email. Keep it
short: most follow-ups are better at 150 words than 400.

Output the draft only. Do not send anything.
