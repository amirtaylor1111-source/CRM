description: Brief me before a call with a person or company
---

Prepare me for a call with: $ARGUMENTS

Read `contacts/<slug>.md` for that person (or every contact at that company),
then read `notes.md` from each meeting linked in their managed Meetings
section, most recent first. Three or four meetings is plenty.

Produce a short briefing:

**Where we left off** — the last conversation in two sentences.

**Outstanding** — action items from previous meetings that are still
unchecked, with who owed what and how long it has been. Be specific about
anything the user themselves promised and has not delivered, since that is
the thing most likely to come up.

**Worth knowing** — durable facts from their contact file: role, preferences,
anything personal they have mentioned.

**Three things to raise** — concrete talking points grounded in what was
actually discussed before, not generic advice.

Keep it to one screen. If there is no history for this person, say so plainly
rather than padding.
