## Meeting Purpose
To introduce branded calling and explore a potential partnership.

## Key Takeaways
- **The Solution:** Network-level branded calling (via First Orion) displays a company name and call reason on a recipient's phone screen *before* they answer, building trust and boosting answer rates.
- **The Opportunity:** A universal product for SCG to resell to all customers (not just mobile), as it works with any outbound number (fixed or mobile) calling a recipient on a supported UK network.
- **UK Coverage:** The solution covers ~72% of UK mobile devices (Vodafone, O2, EE), a market-leading figure.
- **Commercials:** A simple, scalable per-call fee, not per-minute. SCG would get a wholesale rate and reporting to bill customers.
